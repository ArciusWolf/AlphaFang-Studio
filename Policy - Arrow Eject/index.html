Privacy Policy — Arrow Eject

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Developer: Cozy Cave Games — cozycavegames@gmail.com

What Arrow Eject stores

Arrow Eject has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (level, gems, owned arrow styles, settings and the puzzle in progress) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

Ads

The game shows advertising through Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it connects to. To serve and measure ads, these partners may collect and use data from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system, and information about how you interact with ads. We do not control how these partners use that data. See Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

You can limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Analytics

The game does not send analytics to us.

Children

Arrow Eject is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Data deletion

Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. To ask about data held by our ad partners, contact us or the partner directly.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
