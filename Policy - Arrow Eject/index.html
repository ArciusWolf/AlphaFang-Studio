Privacy Policy — Arrow Eject

Effective date: 18 November 2026
Developer: Cozy Cave Games — cozycavegames@gmail.com

What Arrow Eject stores

Arrow Eject has no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (level, gems, owned arrow styles, settings and the puzzle in progress) is saved only on your device. We do not receive it.

Data collected and shared by the app

The game itself does not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or precise location. The third-party services built into the game (listed below) collect the following data from your device:

  • Device or other IDs — such as your Android advertising ID and device identifiers. Used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and shared with ad partners.
  • Approximate location — derived from your IP address (not GPS). Used for advertising and analytics, and shared with ad partners.
  • App interactions — such as ad views, clicks and rewarded ads watched. Used for advertising and analytics, and shared with ad partners.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics — such as device model, operating system version and SDK performance data. Used for analytics and to keep the game and ads working.
  • Purchase history — when you buy gems, a record of the purchase. Used to deliver your purchase and for analytics.

This data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. It is required for the game's ads and purchases to work and cannot be switched off inside the game.

Ads

The game shows advertising through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) and the ad networks it connects to, including Unity Ads. To serve and measure ads, these partners may collect and use the data above. We do not control how these partners use that data. See their privacy policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource, Unity Ads): unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

You can limit ad personalisation, reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

In-app purchases

Gem purchases are processed by Google Play using Unity In-App Purchasing. Payment details are handled by Google; we never see your card or payment information. See Google's privacy policy.

Analytics

We do not run our own analytics servers. Unity's services (ads and in-app purchasing) collect the crash, diagnostic and usage data described above, and we may see it only as aggregated, anonymous reports.

Children

Arrow Eject is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Data retention and deletion

Because your game data is stored only on your device, you can delete it by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Data held by our partners is kept according to their own policies. To reset the identifier they use, reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. To ask for deletion of data held by a partner, contact us at cozycavegames@gmail.com or the partner directly.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
